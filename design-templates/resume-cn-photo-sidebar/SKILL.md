# Photo Sidebar Resume

Design a two-column Chinese resume where the sidebar carries identity, contact, photo placeholder, languages, and compact strengths while the main column carries evidence. Keep the portrait as a neutral generated placeholder, never a real person or copied headshot. Use the format for roles where presentation and trust signals matter, while preserving print clarity.
