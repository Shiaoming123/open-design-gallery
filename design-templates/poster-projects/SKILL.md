# Poster Projects

Use this entry as a source-linked case library for poster work, not as a
bundle of copied campaign assets.

Each entry should preserve the smallest durable signal:

- `project`: title, maker, URL, medium, and format.
- `why`: why the poster is worth keeping.
- `compositions`: focal image logic, type hierarchy, framing, and grid moves.
- `assets`: photography, illustration, typography, print finishes, and support media.
- `motion`: only when a poster is paired with a relevant motion or process artifact.
- `interactions`: only when the poster system includes a meaningful digital extension.
- `implementation`: format, complexity, and evidence limits.
- `capture`: date, source links, attribution, reuse policy, and capture depth.

Use `status: lead` when the source is promising but still needs a stronger
read of the underlying poster set. Use `status: accepted` when the page gives
enough evidence to preserve the reusable poster grammar.
