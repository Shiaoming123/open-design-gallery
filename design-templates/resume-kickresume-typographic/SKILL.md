# Resume Kickresume Typographic

Use this template when the resume should feel designed by typography rather than decoration. Create generous white space, clear labels, strong role titles, and a compact sidebar for proof points. Keep all visual choices original and do not reproduce source templates, typographic pairings, thumbnails, or product copy.
