# Campus Intern Resume

Design a fresh but restrained resume for Chinese campus recruiting and internship applications. Put education, target role, coursework, campus projects, awards, and internship readiness ahead of long career history. Use light accents, clear score/award callouts, and concise bullets that show initiative, teamwork, and measurable project outcomes.
