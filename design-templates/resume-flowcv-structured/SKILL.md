# Resume FlowCV Structured

Use this template for a clean, builder-style resume that values scan speed, text hierarchy, and restrained customization. Keep the page mostly monochrome with one calm accent, put contact and skills in a narrow rail, and make experience bullets specific, metric-led, and easy to parse. Avoid decorative assets, complex graphics, and brand-specific layout cues from any source site.
