# Resume Academic CV Lite

Use this template for graduate students, postdocs, lecturers, and research professionals who need an academic CV that still previews well as a single-page template. Use formal hierarchy, publication-friendly typography, and clear categories for appointments, research, selected publications, teaching, grants, and service. Keep ornament minimal and citation lines readable.
