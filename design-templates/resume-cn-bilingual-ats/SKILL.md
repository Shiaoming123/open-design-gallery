# Bilingual ATS Resume

Design a bilingual Chinese-English resume optimized for applicant tracking systems and human recruiters. Use one text-first column, explicit section labels, no icons, no images, and parallel Chinese/English phrasing where it helps international roles. Keep dates, titles, company names, and skills easy to parse.
