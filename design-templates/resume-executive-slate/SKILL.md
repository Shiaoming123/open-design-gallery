# Resume Executive Slate

Use this template for senior operators, founders, and directors who need fast executive scanning. Lead with scope, mandate, and business outcomes; keep the page composed and confident. Use a narrow slate rail for contact and leadership domains, with the main column reserved for impact narratives, board-ready metrics, and selected transformation work.
