# Mobile App Flow Projects

Keep `references/catalog.json#entryFiles` as the shard index. Each accepted entry belongs
under `references/entries/` and records a task goal, ordered screen sequence,
transitions, platform constraints, exceptional states, source links,
attribution, reuse policy, and capture depth. Never store copied app screens.
