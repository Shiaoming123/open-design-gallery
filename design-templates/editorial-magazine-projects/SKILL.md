# Editorial Magazine Projects

Use this entry as a source-linked case library for editorial systems, not as a
bundle of copied spreads or publication assets.

Each entry should preserve the smallest durable signal:

- `project`: title, maker, URL, medium, and format.
- `why`: why the editorial system is worth keeping.
- `compositions`: cover logic, spread pacing, hierarchy, and grid moves.
- `assets`: typography, illustration, photography direction, and section markers.
- `motion`: only when the publication meaningfully extends into digital motion.
- `interactions`: only when a digital editorial extension adds meaningful navigation or reading behavior.
- `implementation`: format, complexity, and evidence limits.
- `capture`: date, source links, attribution, reuse policy, and capture depth.

Use `status: lead` when the source is promising but still needs a stronger
read of the publication system. Use `status: accepted` when the page gives
enough evidence to preserve the reusable editorial grammar.
