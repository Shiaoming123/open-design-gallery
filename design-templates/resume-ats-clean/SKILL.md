# Resume ATS Clean

Use this template when clarity, scanner compatibility, and conservative hiring workflows matter most. Keep the layout one column, avoid decorative graphics, use explicit section headings, and prioritize measurable accomplishments over visual flourish. The preview should print cleanly on A4 or Letter with high contrast text, plain bullets, predictable dates, and no information hidden in images or icons.
