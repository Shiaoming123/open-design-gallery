# Motion Interaction Projects

Use this entry as a source-linked case library for immersive motion work, not
as a bundle of copied effects.

Each entry should preserve the smallest durable signal:

- `project`: title, maker, URL, medium, and format.
- `why`: why the reference matters.
- `compositions`: stage and section structures worth studying.
- `assets`: the asset types the motion system depends on.
- `motion`: motion patterns plus fallback notes when known.
- `interactions`: cursor, pointer, menu, scroll, and reveal behaviors.
- `implementation`: format, complexity, and evidence limits.
- `capture`: date, source links, attribution, reuse policy, and capture depth.

Use `status: backfill-needed` when the strongest behavior lives behind JS,
canvas, or runtime-only interaction that still needs a live follow-up pass.
