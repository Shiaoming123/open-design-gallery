# Resume Novoresume One Page

Use this template for candidates who need everything to fit on one polished page. Prioritize strong section labels, concise bullets, a compact skills matrix, and a clear reverse-chronological spine. Keep decoration minimal and original; never reproduce commercial thumbnails or named templates.
