## Distilled

Novoresume emphasizes one-page, ATS-friendly, structured templates with strong categories, custom layout options, and compact professional presentation. This template distills those generalized patterns into an original single-page layout with a brief summary strip, skills matrix, and reverse-chronological experience stack.
