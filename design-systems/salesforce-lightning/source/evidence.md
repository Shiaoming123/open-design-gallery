## Distilled Observations

- SLDS is positioned as a flexible UI framework for Salesforce apps, with theming and designer/developer tooling.
- Foundations cover color, typography, spacing and sizing, borders, and iconography as reusable system primitives.
- Color guidance treats color as foundational for meaning, usability, and brand consistency.
- Typography guidance emphasizes consistent heading styles and text colors to guide users through content and preserve visual balance.
- Styling hooks are CSS variables for colors, typography, spacing, sizing, borders, and related design properties.
- The official Salesforce UX repository describes SLDS as tailored for building Salesforce apps whose UI reflects the Lightning look and feel.
